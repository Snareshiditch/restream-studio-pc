## 💡 Best Tips

- ✅ Use 6 Mbps upload per 1080p stream
- ✅ Set bitrate between 4500-6000 kbps
- ✅ Use NVENC for GPU encoding
- ✅ Pre-record intros and outros
- ✅ Follow community guidelines

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

| **Problem** | **Solution** |
|--------------|---------------|
| **Dropped frames** | Lower bitrate |
| **Audio delay** | Sync in OBS settings |
| **Platform disconnects** | Re-link account |
| **Chat not loading** | Refresh browser |
| **Low quality** | Increase upload speed |
| **Studio lagging** | Use OBS instead |
